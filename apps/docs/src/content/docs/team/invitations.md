## Invite someone

**Settings → Team & roles → Invite member** (Admin): their email and a role. They get an email link that expires in **7 days**; pending invitations can be **Resent** or **Revoked**.

Following the link, they create their account (name and password) and join with the role you picked, or **Decline invite**.

## Remove a member

**Remove** ends their access at once and signs them out everywhere. Their name stays on what they did in the audit log. Inviting the same email later brings their account back.
